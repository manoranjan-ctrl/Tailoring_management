Measuring Guide & Reference

For the most comfortable fit, measure over light undergarments using a flexible tailoring measuring tape.

•
Chest: Wrap tape under armpits around the fullest part of your chest.
•
Shoulder: Measure from tip of left shoulder bone across to right shoulder bone.
•
Waist: Measure at natural waistline, typically an inch above navel.
